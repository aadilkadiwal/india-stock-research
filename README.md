# India Stock Research

AI-generated concall (earnings-call) analysis reports for Indian listed companies.

> ⚠️ **Not investment advice.** These reports are generated by an AI research assistant for study and educational purposes only. They may contain errors or omissions. Do your own research and consult a SEBI-registered advisor before making any investment decision.

## What's in here

Each PDF reads a single company's last **2–3 years of earnings-call transcripts** and answers two questions:

1. **Is there a durable earning trigger?** — a real, repeatable driver of future earnings (capacity, order book, new products, margin levers), not a one-off.
2. **Has management been consistent about it?** — promised vs. delivered, quarter over quarter, and whether the story has drifted over time.

Transcripts are sourced from [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in/). One PDF per company.

## Structure

Reports are organized by fiscal quarter:

```
Q4-2026/
  accent-microcell-ltd.pdf
  acutaas-chemicals-ltd.pdf
  ...
```

- `Q4-2026/` — 102 company reports covering the Q4 FY2026 earnings season.

Filenames are the company name, lower-cased and hyphenated (e.g. `granules-india-ltd.pdf`).

## How reports are generated

These are produced with a **concall-tracker** research workflow that:

- pulls the available concall transcripts for a company from Screener.in,
- extracts the recurring narrative and checks it against reported results, and
- summarizes the earning trigger and management-consistency read into a focused PDF.

If a company has no concalls available, the workflow falls back to a broader web deep-dive.
